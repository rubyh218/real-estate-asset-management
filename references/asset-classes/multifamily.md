# Multifamily Asset Management

## What's distinctive

Multifamily is a high-velocity leasing business — leases turn every 12 months on average, so revenue management decisions compound quickly. Operations matter more than in long-WALT commercial classes.

## Key metrics

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Effective rent** | Asking rent − concessions amortized over lease term | Apples-to-apples rent comparison across markets and concessions levels |
| **Trade-out** | New lease rent vs. expiring lease rent for same unit type | Leading indicator of market direction; distinct from renewal % |
| **Renewal rate** | % of expiring leases that renew | Captures resident retention; lower = higher turn costs |
| **Loss to Lease (LTL)** | (Market − In-place) ÷ Market, weighted | Upside opportunity in next 12 months; high LTL = embedded rent growth |
| **Bad debt %** | Bad debt ÷ GPR | Credit risk; trending up = leading indicator of recession or screening issues |
| **Concessions % of GPR** | Concessions ÷ GPR | Captures soft-leasing markets; can mask "asking rent" optics |
| **RUBS recovery** | Utility reimbursements ÷ Utility expense | Operating efficiency; should be 50-90% |
| **Turn cost / unit** | Make-ready cost per move-out | Tracks deferred maintenance and rehab spend |

## Standard reporting cuts

- **By floor plan**: occupancy, asking rent, effective rent, trade-out, LTL per unit type
- **Concessions analysis**: % of new leases with concessions, average months given, weighted across portfolio
- **Renewal pricing**: by lease vintage, by unit type
- **Lease expiration ladder**: monthly, rolling 12 months — flag concentrations (>10% in one month)

## Common business plans

- **Value-add renovations**: interior package (kitchens, baths, flooring) at $5-20k/unit, targeting $50-200/unit rent premium. Track premiums achieved vs. UW.
- **Operational repositioning**: new PM, better revenue management software (LRO, YieldStar, RealPage AI Revenue Management), tighter screening, expense optimization
- **RUBS implementation** (ratio utility billing): conversion to RUBS can add $30-80/unit/month in recovery income
- **Amenity additions**: package rooms, smart access (Latch, RealPage Smart), pet parks, business centers — usually $200-1,000/unit invested

## Asset-class-specific risks

- **Rent control / regulation**: NY, CA, OR, MN, WA, NJ all have stabilization or caps. Critical to know which rules apply.
- **Property tax reassessment**: most states reassess on sale; Texas in particular sees aggressive reassessments and complex appeal processes.
- **Insurance — coastal / CAT**: Florida, Texas Gulf, California fire — premiums up 50-200% in 2022-2024. Critical line item.
- **Supply concentration**: submarket-level supply is the dominant rent growth driver short-term; deliveries within 1-mile radius matter.
- **Operating margin compression**: rising payroll, insurance, and tax in a flat-rent environment can compress margins 200-400 bps.

## Quick performance diagnostic

If asked "how is this multifamily property doing," check in order:

1. **Physical occupancy** vs. submarket
2. **Trade-out** (positive = growing rents; negative = concessions or weak demand)
3. **Renewal rate** (>50% healthy; <40% problematic)
4. **Bad debt %** trend (1-2% typical; >3% concerning)
5. **OpEx / unit** trend, especially insurance and property tax

## Reporting one-liners

- "We grew effective rent 3.2% YoY despite 1.5 months average concessions" — captures both pricing and softness
- "Renewal rate slipped from 54% to 48% as new lease pricing in the submarket outpaced our renewal increases" — actionable
- "LTL declined from 6.8% to 3.1% as we captured ~$45/unit on lease trade-outs YTD" — value harvested
