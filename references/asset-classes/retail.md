# Retail Asset Management

## What's distinctive

Retail performance is tenant-by-tenant: a strong anchor and the right tenant mix create traffic that compounds across the shopping center; a single store closure can trigger cotenancy clauses, occupancy clauses, and a cascade of value loss. Asset management is more *merchandising* than in any other property type.

## Key metrics

| Metric | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales PSF** | Tenant sales ÷ leased SF | Measures tenant health and rent capacity (rent should be ~5-12% of sales for most tenant types) |
| **Occupancy Cost Ratio** | (Rent + CAM + Tax + Insurance) ÷ Sales | <10% healthy; >15% stressed |
| **Anchor** | Typically 25k+ SF dominant tenant (grocer, big box, theater) | Drives traffic to the rest of the center |
| **Co-tenancy provisions** | Right of small tenants to rent reduction or termination if anchor leaves | Asymmetric downside |
| **Percentage rent** | Rent kicker above a breakpoint of sales | Upside in strong performance |
| **CAM (Common Area Maintenance)** | Recoverable expense pool | Recovery rates, caps, exclusions matter — read leases |
| **Traffic** | Foot traffic counts (Placer.ai, etc.) | Leading indicator of tenant sales |

## Center types and their characteristics

| Format | Anchor type | Trade area | Margin profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Strip / neighborhood** | Grocery or drug | 1-3 mile | Stable, defensive |
| **Power center** | Big-box (Costco, Target, TJX, Home Depot) | 5-10 mile | Tenant-rotation risk |
| **Lifestyle / open-air** | Apparel, F&B, experiential | 10-20 mile | Higher cap, higher growth |
| **Mall (regional / super-regional)** | Department stores | 20-50 mile | Highly tenant-dependent; bifurcated quality |
| **Mixed-use / urban retail** | Varies | Local | Driven by foot traffic and surrounding density |
| **Single-tenant NNN ("STNL")** | One tenant on long-term lease | N/A | Bond-like; credit-driven |

## Standard reporting cuts

- **Anchor performance**: sales trend (when reportable), traffic, contractual position (own vs. lease), term
- **In-line tenant sales** (when reportable, typically required in leases): sales PSF and OCR trend
- **Tenant mix**: by category — apparel, F&B, services, fitness, entertainment — track diversification
- **Tenant credit**: national vs. regional vs. local; investment-grade vs. private; track watch list (especially in B malls)
- **Expiration ladder** with renewal probability
- **Percentage rent** earned by tenant, vs. fixed rent

## Common business plans

- **Re-merchandising**: replace underperforming tenants with concepts driving more traffic (often F&B, experiential, services)
- **Outparcel / pad development**: most open-air centers have monetizable outparcels (pad sites for QSR, banks, urgent care)
- **Anchor backfill**: most consequential lever — finding the right replacement for a Sears, Macy's, or grocer departure
- **Densification**: at high-density centers, adding residential or office above the retail
- **Adaptive reuse**: in dying mall situations, conversion to industrial, medical, or residential

## Asset-class-specific risks

- **Anchor failure cascade**: anchor goes dark → cotenancy triggers → in-line rent reductions or terminations → income loss → debt covenant trip
- **Tenant credit**: retail bankruptcies are common (e.g., Bed Bath & Beyond, Express, Christmas Tree Shops); even strong centers see periodic tenant losses
- **E-commerce share**: continues to take share from physical, especially in soft goods; categories vary (groceries: low share; apparel: high)
- **CAM under-recovery**: opex inflates (insurance, tax, R&M) but CAM caps and exclusions limit pass-through. Tracks tightly with NOI margin.
- **Cap rate dispersion**: trophy lifestyle and grocery-anchored = institutional bid; B/C mall = thin bid, distressed-only

## Quick performance diagnostic

1. **Anchor health and term** — single biggest factor
2. **In-line occupancy and recent leasing activity** — direction matters more than level
3. **Sales PSF and OCR trends** — tenant health
4. **Co-tenancy exposure** — if anchor leaves, what cascades?
5. **CAM recovery ratio trend** — captures opex pressure
6. **Tenant watch list** — bankruptcies and store closings on roll

## Reporting one-liners

- "Grocer anchor's reported sales of $850/SF (vs. $700/SF center average) and 18-year term provide durable foot traffic; in-line OCR at 8.2% supports rent growth headroom"
- "Macy's go-dark triggers cotenancy reductions estimated at $1.4M annual NOI exposure across 6 in-line tenants; we are negotiating early termination + redevelopment plan"
- "CAM recovery declined from 92% to 86% as insurance premiums grew 35% YoY against tenant caps; new leases include uncapped insurance pass-through"
